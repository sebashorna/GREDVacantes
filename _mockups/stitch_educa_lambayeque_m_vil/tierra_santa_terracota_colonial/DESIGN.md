## Brand & Style

This design system embraces an earthy, cultural, yet modern civic aesthetic inspired by the Lambayeque region. Rooted in the warm baked clays of Mochica ceramics, the adobe of archaeological sanctuaries, and coastal dry-forest olive groves, it bridges historical pride with contemporary civic utility.

The personality balances civic authority with warm communal hospitality:
- **Tone:** Grounded, welcoming, culturally conscious, transparent, and empowering.
- **Audience:** Students, educators, families, and citizens of Lambayeque engaging in educational modules, civic participation, and regional heritage discovery.
- **Emotional Response:** Comfort, pride of place, approachability, and clarity without clinical detachment.

The visual style merges modern minimalist UI with warm, tactile editorial sensibilities. It avoids sterile corporate grays, replacing them with breathable linen and ivory canvases, warm clay primary elements, and organic olive badges for achievements and civic status.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the adobe architectures and natural environment of northern Peru:

- **Primary (`#A0472E` - Terracota Arcilla):** The foundational brand color used for dominant actions, primary navigation targets, interactive highlights, and focused indicators. It delivers grounded authority with human warmth.
- **Secondary (`#DDB892` - Arena Cálido):** A softened, desert-sand accent for subtle cards, auxiliary highlights, progress meters, and contextual grouping containers.
- **Tertiary (`#58644A` - Olivo Algarrobo):** A muted green invoking northern coastal flora, reserved for educational progress indicators, success validations, civic achievements, and nature-linked modules.
- **Neutral (`#2B2625` - Pardo Sombra):** A deep roasted-earth dark tone that provides high legibility without the harshness of pitch black.

### Surface and Canvas Hierarchy
- **Base Canvas:** Soft linen ivory (`#FBF9F5`) to evoke warm parchment and reduce eye fatigue during long reading and learning sessions.
- **Card & Sheet Surfaces:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle warmth to lift content cleanly over the ivory canvas.
- **Subtle Surface Tiers:** Tinted sand (`#F5EFEB`) for inactive panels, search bars, and table headers.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to project a friendly, approachable, and clearly legible civic interface:

- **Headlines:** Use weights `700` and `800` with slight negative tracking to produce solid editorial grounding without aggression.
- **Body:** Standardized at `400` with generous line-heights (1.6x) to facilitate continuous reading on modest mobile displays and across varying lighting conditions.
- **Labels & Badges:** Weighted at `600` and `700` with positive letter tracking for legibility on small tags, module chips, and navigation labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt spatial grid system tailored for a mobile-first civic application:

- **Mobile (Phone):** 4-column layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem`. Cards and interactive lists prioritize full-width tap surfaces and thumb-reachable navigation.
- **Tablet:** 8-column layout with `margin-tablet: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`, allowing dual-pane master-detail educational modules.
- **Desktop / Web Portal:** 12-column layout centered at a max width of `1200px` with `margin-desktop: 2.5rem` and `gutter-desktop: 1.5rem`.

Dynamic vertical rhythm ensures that educational articles, forms, and civic dashboards retain readable line lengths and clear touch targets (minimum 48x48px interactive boundaries).

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain an organic and accessible feel, this design system relies on **tonal contrast** paired with warm, diffused **ambient shadows** rather than high-contrast synthetic drops:

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Used for base page backgrounds (`#FBF9F5`) and inset fields (`#F5EFEB`).
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Surfaces):** Pure white elements on linen, backed by a subtle warm shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(160, 71, 46, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(43, 38, 37, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Tiles):** Educational task cards and raised buttons: `0 6px 16px -4px rgba(160, 71, 46, 0.12), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(43, 38, 37, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Action Sheets & Drawers):** Elevated dialogs and sticky civic consultation sheets: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(43, 38, 37, 0.18)`.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Soft sandstone borders (`rgba(221, 184, 146, 0.4)`) define sections without visual noise.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy employs `roundedness: 2`, delivering friendly, inviting corners that echo the smoothed contours of Lambayeque pottery and coastal adobe bricks:

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Chips):** `0.5rem` (8px) radius, providing a balanced, approachable touch target.
- **Cards & Task Containers (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) radius, softening content modules and civic announcements.
- **Bottom Sheets & Modal Panels (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) radius on top edges to communicate soft sheet physics.
- **Pill Badges:** Complete pill roundness (`9999px`) for progress indicators, category badges, and user status pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#A0472E`, text white, `height: 48px`, `border-radius: 0.5rem`, font `label-lg`. Pressed state transitions to a deepened terracotta tone (`#853721`).
- **Secondary (Terracota Soft):** Background `rgba(160, 71, 46, 0.08)`, text `#A0472E`, border `1px solid rgba(160, 71, 46, 0.2)`.
- **Tertiary / Civic Action:** Background `#58644A`, text white for completed educational tracks and verified citizen submissions.

### Chips & Tags
- Height `32px`, `border-radius: 9999px`, horizontal padding `0.75rem`.
- **Active Filter:** Sand `#DDB892` background with dark text `#2B2625`.
- **Civic Status / Academic Progress:** `#58644A` background tint with dark olive text `#323B2A`.

### Cards
- Resting on `#FFFFFF` over `#FBF9F5` base. Padding `1rem` to `1.25rem`. Rounded corners `1rem`. 
- Subtle outline: `1px solid rgba(221, 184, 146, 0.35)`.
- Feature cards may include a 4px primary terracotta left-border accent for urgent civic alerts.

### Input Fields
- `height: 48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid rgba(221, 184, 146, 0.6)`, `border-radius: 0.5rem`.
- Focus state: border turns `#A0472E` with a subtle focus glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(160, 71, 46, 0.15)`).

### Lists & Selection Controls
- **Lists:** Inset lists with 1px soft sand dividers. Rows measure minimum `56px` height for thumb comfort.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Terracota `#A0472E` checked state with a clean white inner check or dot; unselected state has a `1.5px` border in `#DDB892`.

### Regional Civic Modules
- **Learning Milestone Pill:** Olive `#58644A` indicator paired with percentage completion in sand.
- **Citizen Voice Sheet:** Bottom-anchored dialogue card with top-radius `1.5rem`, providing structured forms for local education and community feedback.
